# Related work and the scope of the claimed improvement

S. Fettes, R. L. Kramer and S. P. Radziszowski proved the upper bound 62 in
*An Upper Bound of 62 on the Classical Ramsey Number R(3,3,3,3)*,
Ars Combinatoria **72** (2004), 41–63.
[Publisher's paper](https://combinatorialpress.com/article/ars/Volume%20072/volume-72-paper-5.pdf).

Radziszowski's *Small Ramsey Numbers*, revision 18 dated April 24, 2026,
records 51 ≤ R(3,3,3,3) ≤ 62 in §6.1.1 and attributes the upper bound to
that paper.
[Survey](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/DS1/pdf/),
[DOI and revision history](https://doi.org/10.37236/21).

The present result lowers the recorded upper bound by one. Its substantive
ingredients are the supplied-root A/B/D exclusions, a global incidence and
synchronization argument, the guarded G finite exclusion, and an exhaustive
456-formula C cover. The complete argument is in [proof.md](../proof.md).

The critical K15/K16 classifications are known results for which the project
supplies its own local Lean proofs and finite certificates; see
[CLASSIFICATION_PROVENANCE.md](CLASSIFICATION_PROVENANCE.md).

The verification method and its precise comparison with the 2004 computation
are described in [TRUST_AND_CERTIFICATION.md](TRUST_AND_CERTIFICATION.md).
